How the demo calculates

TopicAssumption in this demo
Currency and unitsAll amounts in Saudi riyals (SAR). Rates are percentages of the amount; fees are flat SAR amounts.
L/C opening or increase¼% on the first SAR 400,000 plus ⅛% on the excess, covering the first 3 months; plus 1/16% of the full amount for each further month. For an increase, the amount is the increase only.
L/C periodMonths, rounded up to whole months (3.2 months → 4). Up to 3 months costs the same as exactly 3.
L/C extension1/16% of the full L/C amount per month of extension, rounded up to whole months.
L/C advising / other amendmentAdvising: SAR 20 flat. Other amendment: the fee you enter (SAR 25 by default) plus telex or mail costs.
Letter of guaranteeAnnual rate of ¼% on the first SAR 10,000,000 plus ⅛% on the excess, pro-rated by days ÷ 365, or by whole months ÷ 12 (part months rounded up).
Minimum feesL/C: only the bank minimum you enter (none by default). Guarantee: optional SAR 25 floor, applied before the optional SAR 20 missing bill-of-lading fee.
VATApplied to the subtotal at the rate you enter (15% by default).
RoundingNo rounding inside the calculation; figures are shown rounded to 2 decimals. A difference under SAR 0.01 counts as a match.

Rates as used by the original tool, which cites SAMA, Fees Guide for Financial Institutions’ Services. Edition and effective date not verified for this demo.
